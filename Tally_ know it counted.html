What’s real and what’s mocked

The rules are real. The data feeds are not.

  • Real: arithmetic validation, count-based reports, match or unmatched logic, hash-chained log.
  • Mocked: card status and polling-unit lookup, the published IReV figures, other voters’ reports, WhatsApp and SMS delivery.
  • We haven’t confirmed that INEC offers public data access for these, so a live version needs their cooperation.
