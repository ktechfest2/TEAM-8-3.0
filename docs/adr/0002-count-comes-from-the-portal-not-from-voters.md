# The count comes from the results portal, not from figures voters send in

The first version asked voters to type in the figures from the result sheet on the wall and treated two identical submissions as agreement. We removed that. The count trail is built only from the results INEC publishes per polling unit on its portal.

Voter-typed figures can be mistyped or invented, and two people agreeing proves little. The portal figure is the election authority's own publication, so a mismatch between its sum and the announced result compares INEC with INEC. That is a stronger position than voters against INEC, and it needs nobody at the polling unit.

## Consequences

- Ward results are never announced publicly, so ward portal sums are shown as working and the first real comparison is at local-government level.
- The portal publishes images, not data. Tally depends on someone reading the sheets into figures. The demo uses the 2023 Rivers State figures read by CCIJ; a live election needs an equivalent feed.
- Voters still send live reports about what is happening at their polling unit. Those were never part of the count.
